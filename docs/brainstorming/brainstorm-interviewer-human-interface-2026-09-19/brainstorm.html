LENS 01

Empathy Map

16 IDEAS

Deep at 9am with three features in flight. He says what is the status and did it ask me something. He thinks is it stuck or thinking, and I could have done this myself by now. He does constant alt-tabbing and diff-skimming between meetings. He feels low-grade spend anxiety plus guilt about not reviewing properly.

“
SAYS
“What’s the status — and did it ask me something?”
  • Status must be answerable in under two seconds WITHOUT reading — one glanceable line per feature. If a paragraph is needed to know the state, the interface has already failed.
  • Answer in the terminal he is already in. The web app is for LOOKING, the terminal is for ANSWERING — never force a context switch to reply.
  • Make just-do-it a real first-class command: stop asking, use judgment, I will review at the end.
  • Make rejection cheap — one keystroke plus a reason, and that reason becomes a decision-ledger entry automatically.
DEEP · MONDAY
09:00
checkout-retry ? asking auth-sso merged 03:14 payments-hook blocked
Between two meetings. Six browser tabs. Half a coffee. The mental model of all three features decays before he gets back.
◎
THINKS
“Is it stuck, or thinking? I could have done this myself by now.”
  • Visually distinguish stuck from thinking — that ambiguity is the single largest source of anxiety. A heartbeat pulse naming the current step, never an undifferentiated spinner.
  • His three-feature mental model decays fast between meetings, so every notification must reload the context itself rather than assuming he still holds it.
  • Every question carries its own mini-brief, so it is answerable without loading the full feature context back into his head.
  • Show what he actually asked for alongside what is being built — at 9am he does not remember his own 4pm phrasing.
⇄
DOES
Alt-tab. Skim. Alt-tab. Meeting. Repeat.
  • Never interrupt with anything that cannot be resolved in ten seconds — anything longer gets queued for a boundary.
  • Batch interruptions to natural boundaries with a do-not-disturb-until mode that queues questions rather than firing them into a meeting.
  • He skims diffs rather than reading them, so the system must say WHERE to look — the risky twelve lines of a four-hundred-line diff. Review guidance, not review dumps.
  • I-could-have-done-this-myself is the churn signal: surface elapsed-versus-estimate continuously to enable killing early instead of sunk-costing.
♥
FEELS
Low-grade spend anxiety. Guilt about not reviewing properly.
  • Cost needs a permanently visible running number — spend anxiety is continuous, and a report he has to request does not relieve it.
  • The system must state its own confidence AND what it did not verify, so trust is calibrated rather than blind and the guilt about skipping review is resolved honestly.
  • Target the emotional register of a competent colleague reporting in, not a dashboard demanding attention.
  • Silence must be a trustworthy contract — no news is good news — or he will poll anxiously and the whole ambient design collapses.

LENS 04

Seven Metaphors

20 DONATIONS

Seven metaphors for the Interviewer, each donating a different mechanism — and the autopilot one donates the most safety-critical concept in the entire session: mode confusion.

CHIEF OF STAFFDELEGATION
KPI
The Interviewer’s real KPI is QUESTIONS NOT ASKED — it should report “I handled it” far more often than it asks “what should I do?”
FORMAT
A standing brief format that never varies, so he learns to read it in one saccade rather than parsing fresh prose each time.
MANDATE
It owns the calendar of his attention and is accountable for spending it well.
AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLCOORDINATION
STRIP
A compact strip per feature holding everything needed to resume it cold.
PHRASEOLOGY
Constrained phraseology — a small fixed vocabulary of message types rather than free prose.
READBACK
READBACK is spec echo. The system restates his instruction before acting on it, and the readback is the contract.
SEPARATION
Separation minima map to conflict domains, and handoff between controllers maps to the artifact passed between phases.
SURGICAL NURSEANTICIPATION
PRE-FETCH
Anticipate the next instrument and have it ready before it is requested — pre-fetch what he will need at the next decision point.
COUNT
The sponge count is a reconciliation checklist that must balance before closing — and nothing merges until it does.
VOICE
Speak up only when something is wrong, which is the purest form of the silence-means-success contract.
NEWSROOM EDITORSTRUCTURE
PYRAMID
Every report follows the inverted pyramid — most important thing first, detail decaying downward.
HEADLINE
Headline discipline: every message carries a one-line headline that stands completely alone.
SOURCING
Sourcing rules map to provenance — no claim without a traceable source.
SOUS CHEF & EXPEDITERRHYTHM
CALL
A short, rhythmic, predictable calling voice, with features as tickets moving through a pass.
JUNIOR DEV, MENTOREDGROWTH
CORRECTION
Correction must be cheap to give AND must stick permanently — which is the decision ledger, justified from the human side.
ROPE
Rope is earned per area, rather than granted globally.
AUTOPILOTSAFETY-CRITICAL
MODE
MODE VISIBILITY is safety-critical — he must always know at a glance whether the system is in autonomous, asking, or paused mode.
CONFUSION
Mode confusion is the classic accident class in aviation, and it is the exact failure waiting in a system with autonomy tiers — name the current mode in the prompt line, permanently.
DISENGAGE
Disengagement must be instant, obvious and always available, with a single physical gesture that always means stop.
CALLOUTS
Standard callouts at phase transitions, so the human builds an accurate mental model of what the machine is doing without asking.
LENS 05

Shadow Work

14 ADMISSIONS

What Deep is actually avoiding is more predictive of adoption than any feature. The fears: owning incomprehensible code, losing the feel of his own codebase, burning money while asleep, and quietly preferring to build the tool rather than the products.

FEAR · COMPREHENSION
Fear of owning code he does not understand means comprehensibility is a hard requirement, not a nicety — the system must write in HIS idiom, and the review artifact must make the diff understandable, not merely correct.
FEAR · LOSS OF FEEL
Fear of losing the feel for his own codebase argues for deliberately keeping him in the loop on architecture while delegating mechanics. The parts he enjoys are a design input, not an inefficiency to optimize away.
SHADOW · PROCRASTINATION
The honest shadow: building the orchestrator is more fun than building the products — so the real success metric must be shipping velocity of actual products, not sophistication of the tool.
FEAR · MONEY
Fear of burning money overnight is resolved not by a cap alone but by a morning receipt, so the anxiety closes daily instead of compounding.
FEAR · REPUTATION
Fear of looking bad when it commits something dumb makes attribution an EMOTIONAL design question — PR by default rather than direct commit, and a clear convention for whose name is on the commit.
IDENTITY
Deep does not want to be the orchestrator’s user. He wants to be a builder with leverage — so the interface must feel like his own capability rather than a product he operates.
ADMISSION · REVIEW
He will not do thorough reviews, because reviewing is boring. So design honestly for that: make verification strong enough that skimming is the RATIONAL choice, rather than building a beautiful review UI he will not open.
FEAR · SILENT DECAY
Fear of silent degradation — it works for three months then quietly gets worse — is exactly what the weekly self-report and the trust meter exist to make visible.
THE SECRET WISH
To describe a feature and go make coffee. Which means the true north metric is percentage of features completed with ZERO mid-flight interruptions.
THE EXCHANGE RATE
An interruption costs roughly fifteen minutes of his focus, so the system should be willing to spend a great deal of compute to avoid one. Quantifying the exchange rate between tokens and attention is the single most clarifying idea for the whole interface.
ADMISSION · BLAME
He will blame the system for his own vague specs — and spec echo protects both parties, because the echo becomes the contract.
RISK · UNCANNY REGISTER
Too human a question is unsettling; too robotic a question gets ignored. Aim squarely at colleague — not butler, not robot.
RISK · RESENTMENT
If the system asks him to do something tedious that it could have done, trust drops faster than it does from an outright bug.
THE OTHER SECRET WISH
He secretly wants to be impressed. A system that occasionally shows him something he did not know about his own codebase buys more trust than one that is merely correct.

01MASTER VARIABLE
The exchange rate between tokens and attention
An interruption costs about fifteen minutes of focus. So the system should be willing to spend large amounts of compute to avoid a single question. This retroactively settles session one’s token-minimal premise: attention is orders of magnitude more expensive than tokens, and every other rule in this document is downstream of that one price.

ATTENTION SPENT~15 min
SPEND THE FIRST TO PROTECT THE SECOND. ALWAYS.
02DISGUISE
Silence-means-success is a verification requirement wearing an interface costume
Three metaphors independently produced speak only when something is wrong. But that contract is only trustworthy if detection is reliable. The moment the system stays quiet about a failure it did not detect, every ambient design decision in this session collapses at once.

EMPATHYno news is good news
→ THEREFOREdetection must be real
03ACCIDENT CLASS
Mode confusion is the aviation accident already waiting in the design
A system with autonomy tiers has modes, and modes are what pilots crash on. The mitigations are borrowed wholesale: permanent mode display in the prompt line, instant disengage, standard callouts at transitions. Modes are how reversibility gets communicated to the human.

Spec echo and ATC readback are the same mechanism, reached by unrelated routes
Found once by threat-modeling specification drift. Found again by asking what an air traffic controller does. Same answer: restate the instruction before acting, and let the restatement be the contract. Two unrelated routes to one mechanism means it is load-bearing — and it should be built first.

Default-on-timeout makes async tolerable
Every question states what happens if it is ignored. That single mechanic reconciles never block with do not act unapproved, and it makes non-response a valid input rather than a deadlock.
